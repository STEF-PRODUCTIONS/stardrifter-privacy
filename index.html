Star Drifter Privacy Policy
Thank you for playing Star Drifter, developed by Stef Productions.
This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use
the game, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.
By downloading or using Star Drifter, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Star Drifter itself does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or home address. However, third-party services used by the game may automatically collect certain technical information, including but not limited to:
  • Device type and model
  • Operating system version
  • Device identifiers
  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • Language settings
  • Approximate location based on IP address
  • Crash reports and diagnostic information
  • Gameplay performance information necessary to improve the game

2. Advertising

Star Drifter uses Unity Ads to display rewarded advertisements. Unity Ads may collect device information and advertising identifiers in accordance with its own privacy practices in order to:
  • Display advertisements
  • Prevent fraud
  • Measure advertising performance
  • Improve advertising services

For more information, please review Unity's Privacy Policy: Unity Privacy Policy

3. How Information Is Used

Information collected through third-party services may be used to:
  • Operate the game
  • Display rewarded advertisements
  • Improve game stability and performance
  • Diagnose crashes and technical problems
  • Prevent fraudulent or abusive activity

Stef Productions does not sell your personal information.

4. Third-Party Services

Star Drifter may use third-party services including:
  • Unity Ads
  • Google Play Services

These services have their own privacy policies governing the collection and processing of information.

5. Children's Privacy

Star Drifter is not specifically directed toward children under the age required by applicable laws in their country. If you believe personal information has been collected from a child inappropriately, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to help protect information processed through the game. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page together with the updated revision date. Continued use of the game after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
